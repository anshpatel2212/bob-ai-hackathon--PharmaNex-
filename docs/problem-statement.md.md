# Problem Statement

## Background

Pharmaceutical companies must maintain reliable drug-safety information and prepare extensive regulatory submission packages. Pharmacovigilance and regulatory teams work with adverse-event records and large Common Technical Document (CTD) packages that contain information across quality, nonclinical, clinical, and administrative modules.

## The Problem

Reviewing safety data and checking CTD packages for missing or incomplete sections can require substantial manual effort. Teams need to identify potential safety signals, understand their evidence, and determine whether a submission package contains the applicable CTD sections and supporting information. Manual checking across many documents can be difficult to perform consistently and can make it harder to prioritize the most important issues.

## Who is Affected

The primary users are:

- Pharmacovigilance professionals reviewing adverse-event information.
- Regulatory affairs professionals checking submission readiness.
- Clinical safety teams reviewing safety evidence.
- Quality and medical affairs teams contributing to regulatory packages.
- Research and pharmaceutical teams preparing documentation for regulatory review.

## Why It Matters

Missed safety patterns can delay appropriate review, while missing or incomplete regulatory documentation can create submission-readiness issues and additional review cycles. A structured system can help teams organize evidence, prioritize gaps, and reduce repetitive manual checking.

For a hackathon prototype, PharmaGuard AI focuses on making this review workflow faster, more structured, and easier to understand. It does not replace qualified pharmacovigilance or regulatory professionals.

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Traditional workflows often depend on spreadsheets, document-by-document inspection, manually maintained checklists, and separate tools for safety analysis and regulatory documentation. These approaches can make it difficult to maintain a single view of safety findings and submission readiness.

PharmaGuard AI brings these workflows into one platform and adds evidence-based CTD section detection, dynamic gap identification, readiness scoring, and safety intelligence to support human review.
